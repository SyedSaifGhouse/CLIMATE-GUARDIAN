# CLIMATE GUARDIAN

# INTRODUCTION
Climate Guardians is a digital platform designed to promote environmental awareness and responsible habits among school students. It combines education, gamification, and reward-based motivation to create a fun and meaningful experience.

The website achieves several goals:
* Educates students about climate change impacts.
* Encourages eco-friendly habits through a point-based system.
* Students can log various eco-actions:
  
| Activity  | Points |
| ------------- | ------------- |
| Recycled Waste  | +5 |
| 💡 Saved Energy | +5  |
| 🚍 Used Public Transport | +5  |
| 💡 Saved Water | +5  |
| 💡 Saved Energy | +5  |
| ❌ Mistake / Harmful Action | +5  |

*	Allows students to track their progress and stay motivated.
*	Gives teachers administrative control to verify and award points.
*	Creates a sustainable culture within the school community.
With its clean interface, simple navigation, and interactive design, the Climate Guardians platform serves as a powerful educational tool to inspire the next generation of environmental stewards.
PROBLEM STATEMENT

Climate change has become one of the most critical global issues, and creating environmental awareness among young students is essential for building a sustainable future. However, traditional classroom teaching often fails to engage students or motivate them to adopt eco-friendly habits in their daily lives. Schools need an interactive, digital, and student-friendly system that not only educates learners about climate change but also encourages them to practice sustainable actions consistently. There is a lack of platforms that combine education, behaviour-tracking, and reward-based motivation to inspire long-term positive habits among school children.






# OBJECTIVE
The Climate Guardians project aims to:
1. Educate students about climate change, environmental impacts, and sustainability.
2. Motivate students to adopt eco-friendly habits through a point-based rewards system.
3. Track and log students’ day-to-day green actions such as recycling, saving energy, using public transport, and conserving water.
4. Enable teachers/admins to monitor and verify student activities through an admin dashboard.
5. Build a sustainable school community by integrating learning with gamification and responsible behaviour.
6. Create a simple, interactive, and child-friendly digital platform that makes environmental protection fun and meaningful.

## <ins>PROPOSED SOLUTION</ins>
Climate Guardians is a web-based digital eco-awareness and reward platform designed for school students.
The solution integrates education, gamification, and administrative tracking to promote environmental responsibility.
Key Features of the Solution : 

a) Awareness Through Visual Content
A dedicated Home/Impact section displays information about climate change impacts. Covers topics such as extreme weather, droughts, ocean acidification, and biodiversity loss. Uses illustrations and simple explanations for better understanding.

b) Green Points System
Students can log eco-friendly actions, each associated with a specific score:
Eco Action	              Points
Recycled Waste  	         +5
Saved Energy	            +5
Used Public Transport	   +10
Saved Water	               +10
Harmful/Mistake Action 	   –1
This encourages consistent positive behaviour with instant feedback.

c) Student Dashboard
Displays total green points.
Students select eco-actions from a list.
Real-time tracking keeps students motivated.
Personalized welcome messages help enhance engagement.

d) Admin Dashboard
Teachers/administrators can award additional points.
Admin can view student list, update points, refresh data, and logout. Ensures accountability and prevents misuse of points.

e) Gamified Learning Experience
Makes sustainability enjoyable.
Introduces friendly competition among students.
Encourages long-term behaviour change.
